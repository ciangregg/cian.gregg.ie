Probablistic Computing 2026 - CRANN Institute, TCD

QUBO and Ising Formulations of Optimisation Problems

A 12-week SURE (Summer Undergraduate Research Experience, TCD School of Physics) project at the CRANN Institute, supervised by Professor Stefano Sanvito and Michael Mitchell. We reformulated optimisation problems as searches for minimum-energy states of an Ising or QUBO energy landscape, building a Monte Carlo Markov Chain framework and using simulated annealing to explore the landscape and find its minimum. We applied this to a sequence of established combinatorial problems, extending to a mixed-integer optimal control problem of a 1D rocket trajectory. This showed us that such problems can be translated naturally to probabilistic computing hardware.

Black Hole Thermodynamics 2026

The First and Second Laws Following Bekenstein (1973)

A reading project supervised by Manya Sahni O'Hara, deriving black hole thermodynamics from its relativistic foundations. We started with special and general relativity, moving through the Schwarzschild, Reissner-Nordström, and Kerr solutions, and derived the first and second laws following Bekenstein (1973), through to temperature and the generalised second law.

The Problem Solving Association's Hackathon 2026

Stochastic Constraint Grouping through Simulated Annealing

I participated in the TPSA's annual hackathon in collaboration with TF Wind, where my team came second. The project aimed to improve the efficiency of Ireland's electricity grid: Ireland dispatches down a significant share of its renewable generation each year due to curtailment, which the TSO manages by cutting generators in fixed constraint groups.

We applied simulated annealing to the problem, finding dynamic constraint groupings that reduced the dispatch down percentage of renewables. We built a simulation taking in towns and a given grouping to produce a dispatch down rate, then a proposal and annealing schedule to optimise it. Our method reduced dispatch down by 0.5% compared to the current all-Ireland groupings, a saving of millions of euros per year.
